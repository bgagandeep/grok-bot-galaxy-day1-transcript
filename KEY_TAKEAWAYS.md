## Product direction on Day 1 (still fluid at close)
- Strong thread: run a **food pop-up in San Francisco**, dogfood the experience, and productize an **OS / platform for running pop-ups** (operators + public face).
- Crowd / bot-scanned ideas also included indie-author marketing, marketplaces, climate, dinner delivery, potato-themed restaurant riffs, merch (plushies, stress balls, tickets).
- **Honest close:** the idea **shifted a couple of times**. After Jenny’s venue/permit reality check, the team planned to **lock overnight**. Day 2 focus called out: venue-scout emails ready by morning, invite-list sourcing, event-planner bot team (incl. “permit bot”), overnight agents.

## Practical Grok Bot product takeaways demoed
- Bots = **roles / outcomes**, not one-off chats — come back to the same bot; it accumulates memory.
- Each bot gets a **cloud Linux computer** (browser, docs, forms, deploys, computer use).
- **Marketplace** templates + team-published bots for consistency (e.g. Lauren’s Dr. Egg bot).
- Skills: teach once (e.g. slide animation), reuse.
- Trust / auto-review: dial down approval loops as the bot earns autonomy.
- Credentials: computer logins, 1Password, VPN for internal systems.
- Meta pattern: onboarding / “bot factory” bots that hire and brief other bots from a company handbook; Slack + Cursor automations as the software factory.
