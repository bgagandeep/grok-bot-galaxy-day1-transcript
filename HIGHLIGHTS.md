## Practical Grok Bot takeaways demoed
- Bots = **roles / outcomes**, not one-off chats.
- Each bot gets a **cloud Linux computer** (browser, docs, forms, deploys).
- **Marketplace** templates + team-published bots for consistency.
- Skills: teach once (e.g. animation), reuse.
- Memory / trust: dial down approval loops as the bot earns autonomy.
- Credentials: computer logins, 1Password integration, VPN for internal systems.
- Meta pattern: onboarding / “bot factory” bots that hire and brief other bots from a company handbook.
